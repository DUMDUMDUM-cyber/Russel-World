Welcome to Russel World

You are Arlo, a kid from Mossbrook. Leaflets keep raiding the gardens past the town gate. Grab your stick and see what you can do.

Controls

  • Move: click or tap the ground. Your hero always runs.
  • Attack: click a monster. Your hero runs over and attacks on their own.
  • Pick up: click things that monsters drop.
  • Talk: click a person with a green name. Mira sells potions.
  • Camera: drag to turn (right-drag or two fingers also work), scroll or pinch to zoom, Q and E to turn.
  • Skills: keys 1–6 or tap the buttons. R for the finishing move when the star circle is full.
  • B bag, C stats, J quest book, M world map, K monster book, P pet, G equipment (Bolt in Mossbrook sells, forges and repairs gear), X sit and rest. HP and MP come back fast inside Mossbrook.
  • Quests: people with a yellow ! have something to ask, a yellow ? means you can report back. The yellow star on the map shows where to go.
  • Jobs: at Lv. 10 four masters teach Warrior, Archer, Mage and Robot. Spend skill points in the stats window (C). T turns auto hunt on or off.
  • Pets: Pip in Mossbrook gives you a mystery egg. Carry it and it hatches. Feed, play with and bathe your pet (P) so it stays happy and fights well.
  • Travel: walk through a gate with a glowing swirl to reach the next area. Treasure chests are hidden in every area.
